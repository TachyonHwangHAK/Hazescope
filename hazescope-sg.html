      What to do

      MOH and NEA guidance by 24-hour PSI. Your current band is highlighted.

      24-hr PSI Healthy persons Elderly, pregnant women, children Chronic lung or heart disease
      • Use the 1-hour PM2.5 reading for decisions right now, such as going for a run. Use the 24-hour PSI for planning the next day.
      • N95 masks are not needed for short exposures such as walking to a bus stop. Healthy persons: consider one when PSI is forecast above 300; persons with chronic lung or heart disease: above 200.
      • Seek medical attention if you feel unwell. In an emergency, call 995.

        About these numbers

        HazeScope SG is an independent viewer of public data and is not affiliated with NEA or MSS. For official advisories, refer to haze.gov.sg and weather.gov.sg.

        Air quality and weather: NEA & MSS via data.gov.sg (Singapore Open Data Licence). Regional model: Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS) via Open-Meteo.
